# Recording setup

Both videos are screen recording plus two people on camera. This is not a cinematography exercise —
clear and readable beats pretty. Budget a half day to record both.

## Screen capture

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | 1920×1080 | Anything higher makes text tiny on playback |
| Frame rate | 30fps | 60 is wasted on screen content |
| Browser zoom | 125–150% | Most people watch courses on a laptop or phone |
| Cursor | Highlight/click effects ON | Viewers lose the pointer constantly |

**Before recording:** close every other tab, quit Slack/Discord/Mail, turn on Do Not Disturb, hide
the bookmarks bar, empty a messy desktop, and log out of anything with a personal name in it.

## Audio — the thing that actually matters

Viewers forgive bad video. They quit over bad audio.

- One mic per person, close (6–10 inches). A $60 USB mic is fine.
- Record each presenter to a **separate track** so you can fix one person's levels.
- Soft room. Carpet, curtains, not a kitchen.
- Do a 20-second test recording and *listen back on headphones* before recording 20 minutes.
- Target around −16 LUFS, peaks under −3 dB.

## Camera

- Phone on a tripod is fine. Eye level, not below.
- Window in front of you, not behind.
- Frame chest-up with a little headroom, both presenters in one shot or two clean singles.
- Lock exposure and focus so it doesn't hunt mid-sentence.

## Practical

- **Record in segments**, section by section from the run of show. Do not attempt one 20-minute take.
- Clap once at the start of every segment for easy sync.
- If you flub, pause, clap, and redo the sentence. Don't restart the section.
- Record the cold open **last**, once you know what the video actually became.

## Editing

- Cut dead air and long queue waits, but acknowledge them first ("these take about ninety seconds")
- Zoom in on the screen whenever anything small matters — text, a setting, a filename
- Burn captions. A large share of viewers watch muted.
- Chapter markers at every timestamp in the run of show
- Export 1080p, H.264, high bitrate

## Delivery specs

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Format | MP4, H.264, 1080p |
| Target size | Under 2 GB per video |
| Captions | Burned in, plus an .srt sidecar if the platform supports it |
| Naming | `01-identity-lock.mp4`, `02-full-build.mp4` |
