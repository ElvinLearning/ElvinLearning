# Packaging and delivery

## What the buyer receives

```
Same Face, Every Shot/
├─ START-HERE.pdf                  one page: what to do first
├─ Book 1 — The Playbook.pdf       11 pages
├─ Book 2 — The Vault.pdf          12 pages
├─ Book 3 — The Business.pdf        9 pages
├─ Video 1 — Identity Lock.mp4     ~20 min
├─ Video 2 — The Full Build.mp4    ~20 min
└─ Templates/                      (order bump only)
   ├─ shot-graph.xlsx
   ├─ shot-templates.txt
   ├─ identity-kit-checklist.pdf
   ├─ client-intake.pdf
   ├─ likeness-release-template.docx
   └─ pricing-calculator.xlsx
```

## START-HERE page — write this before launch

One page, and it should say exactly this in this order:

1. **Read Book One first.** 20 minutes. It's the whole system; nothing else makes sense without it.
2. **Watch Video 1.** Then go shoot your identity kit before watching anything else.
3. **Keep Book Two open** while you work. It's a reference, not a read-through.
4. **Watch Video 2** once you have a kit.
5. **Read Book Three** before you take money from anybody.

The single most common way people waste a purchase like this is consuming it all passively and
never shooting the reference photos. Say that on the page.

## Platform

Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy. Both handle checkout, VAT, file hosting and video streaming, and both let
you update files so buyers get new versions automatically.

Do not build a custom site to launch. If it takes off, migrate then.

## File hygiene

- PDFs: check on a phone before shipping. Small type in a table is fine on a laptop and unreadable on a phone.
- Videos: burned-in captions, plus chapter markers matching the run of show.
- Test the whole purchase flow yourself with a real card before announcing anything.

## Updates

You promised free updates. Make that cheap for yourself:

- The books are built from HTML — edit the body file, run `./build.sh`, re-upload. Two minutes.
- Book Two's model cheat sheet is the part that goes stale fastest. Set a reminder to review it monthly.
- When you update, email buyers. It's a genuine goodwill touch and it puts your DFY offer in their inbox again for free.

## Support

Set expectations on the sales page so this doesn't eat your life:
- Email support, 48-hour response, questions about the material only
- No custom project reviews at this price — that's a paid consult
- Keep an FAQ and add to it every time you answer something twice
