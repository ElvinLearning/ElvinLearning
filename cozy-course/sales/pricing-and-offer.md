# Pricing, offer structure, and the ladder

## Core offer

**Same Face, Every Shot — $100 one time.**
3 PDFs (32 pages) + 2 videos (~40 min).

$100 is a good price for this. It's above impulse — which means buyers actually consume it and get
results — and below the point where people need a sales call. It also positions you correctly: this
is not a $27 ebook, and the people who buy at $100 are the ones who might later hire you.

## The ladder — where the money actually is

The course is not the business. It's the top of the funnel for the business.

| Rung | Offer | Price | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The course | $100 | Volume, credibility, list building |
| 2 | Order bump: Templates Pack | +$27 | Raises average order ~25% for zero extra work |
| 3 | Pilot production | $1,500–3,500 | Warm buyers who'd rather not DIY |
| 4 | Monthly retainer | $4,000–12,000/mo | The actual business |

**Roughly 3–8% of course buyers will ask about done-for-you.** That's the real return on the course.
A hundred sales at $100 is $10,000 — but three of those hundred becoming retainer clients is
$150,000+ a year. Price and promote the course accordingly: it's a lead magnet that happens to be
profitable.

### Make the upsell effortless
- Last page of Book Three: "If you'd rather have this done for you, here's how that works."
- End of Video 2: one clean sentence, no hard pitch.
- Thank-you page after purchase: a booking link.

## Order bump — Templates Pack (+$27)

Everything in a working format instead of a PDF:
- Shot graph spreadsheet, pre-built with the cost formula in it
- All 12 shot templates as copy-paste text
- Identity kit shot list as a printable checklist
- Client intake form
- Likeness release template
- Pricing calculator

This is nearly free to make — it's already written across the three books — and a meaningful share
of buyers take it.

## Decide before launch

- [ ] **Refund policy.** Recommend 14-day, no questions. Digital refund rates run low and it removes the biggest objection. Whatever you choose, put it on the page.
- [ ] **Platform.** Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy = fastest, handles VAT/tax, hosts video. Stan or Kajabi if you want a nicer portal. Don't build a custom site to launch.
- [ ] **License terms.** Personal + commercial use of the *method*; no reselling or redistributing the materials.
- [ ] **Update promise.** Free updates — cheap for you, meaningful to buyers, and it de-risks "will this be obsolete?"

## Pricing traps to avoid

- **Don't discount in week one.** You have no social proof yet; discounting signals the price isn't real.
- **Don't bundle a call at $100.** Your time doesn't scale at this price. Calls belong at rung 3.
- **Don't run a fake countdown.** These buyers are marketers. They'll clock it and you'll lose them.
- **Do raise the price later** once you have testimonials. $100 → $149 is very defensible with results attached.
