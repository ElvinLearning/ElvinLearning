# Launch plan

## Sequencing — this matters more than the copy

**Record the videos after you have produced at least one real video with this system end to end.**

Right now the method is sound but you haven't run it in production. That's fine for the books — they
teach a method — but the videos need to show something real. If you record demos before you've
actually built with it, it shows, and this audience can tell.

Recommended order:

1. **Build one identity kit.** 45 minutes. Yours or Kayson's.
2. **Produce one complete video** with it, end to end. Publish it.
3. **Then record the two course videos** using that same identity — you'll be fluent, and the demos will be real.
4. **Launch.**

That's about a week of work and it makes the difference between a course that sells once and a
course that gets recommended.

## Pre-launch checklist

- [ ] Both videos recorded, edited, captioned, uploaded
- [ ] Three PDFs exported and opened on a phone to check readability
- [ ] Sales page live, checkout tested with a real card
- [ ] Delivery tested — buy it yourself and confirm everything downloads
- [ ] Refund policy written and visible
- [ ] The DFY booking link works
- [ ] At least 3 pieces of proof: real videos you made with the system

## Launch week

| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| **Mon** | Post the best video you've made with the system. No pitch. Just the work. |
| **Tue** | Break down *how* you made it. Free value. Mention a system exists. |
| **Wed** | Open the course. Post the before/after face-drift comparison — it's the strongest visual you have. |
| **Thu** | Answer objections publicly. "Does this work for X?" |
| **Fri** | Show the five-posts-from-one-generation clip. Best single conversion asset. |
| **Sun** | Last call, honestly framed. Don't invent scarcity. |

## The content that sells this

Rank order, best first:

1. **The face-drift before/after.** Four inconsistent faces, then the same face across four shots. This one image does more than any copy you'll write.
2. **The re-roll.** "This shot was wrong. Fixed it for $4 without touching the other eleven."
3. **Five posts from one generation.** The math is the pitch.
4. **A genuinely good finished video** with a caption about how it was made.

Every one of these is a clip from your own course videos. Cut them out and use them as ads.

## If Dr. Boyce is involved

If the co-branded version happens, everything above changes and improves — his audience is the
distribution and his name is the credibility. Do not wait on that to launch. Launch this version
now, get results and testimonials, and bring proof to that conversation. A course already selling is
a much stronger negotiating position than a course you're proposing.
